# Where Weekly Table's numbers come from

*Checked 3 Nov 2026.*

## Calories and protein (home recipes): verified
- **16 dinners:** computed in the 18 Oct 2026 audit from ingredient weights × USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) values per 100 g. On 3 Nov the app values were confirmed to match the audit, and the audit's per-100 g figures were checked against an independent copy of USDA's Standard Reference database (SR28): 46 of 48 ingredients match. The two that differ (whole-milk ricotta, Italian pork sausage) use newer FoodData Central values than the 2015 SR28 file; the difference is at most about 25 kcal per serving (Baked Ziti) and 65 kcal per serving (Sausage and Peppers).
- **8 breakfasts and lunches, banana bread:** recomputed on 3 Nov from SR28 values and stated gram weights (eggs 50 g, bread slice 25 g, peanut butter 16 g per tbsp, and so on). Corrections made: Bean and Cheese Quesadilla 500 → 410 kcal; Yogurt Parfait 260 → 290; Turkey Sandwich 310 → 340; Scrambled Eggs and Toast 320 → 300; others changed by 20 kcal or less. Banana bread (400 kcal per slice) was already correct.
- All values are estimates: real products, brands and portion sizes vary, usually by 10–15%.

## Prices
- **24 of 89 ingredient prices** come from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics average retail prices, South region, September 2026 (iceberg lettuce: U.S. average), converted to recipe units with USDA portion weights. Fixed on 3 Nov: tomatoes were $0.80 each, now $0.54 (a medium tomato is about 123 g at $2.01/lb); bread slices $0.09 → $0.10.
- **65 of 89** are typical store-brand estimates, marked "est" in the app. They have not been checked against a store and should be replaced with real store prices.
- **Eating out** uses a flat $10 per person per meal, an assumption the user can change.

## Restaurant nutrition (Eating Out tab)
Live data from Nom (nom.now) and, with a key, Nutritionix. The 18 Oct audit checked 11 items against restaurant sources: 8 matched, 1 minor difference, 2 could not be checked.

## Calorie Budget
Mifflin-St Jeor formula and step calories, reviewed and corrected in the 18 Oct audit.
